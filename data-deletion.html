Delete your data

Last updated: 19 November 2026

You can delete the data Kreatorkit holds about you at any time. Pick the option that fits you.

If you have a Kreatorkit account

  1. Sign in to Kreatorkit and open Settings.
  2. To remove one platform, click Disconnect next to Instagram, Facebook or YouTube. We stop using it right away and delete its tokens and data within 7 days.
  3. To remove everything, click Delete my account and confirm. We delete your account, connected platforms, pages, settings and stats within 7 days, and from backups within 30 days.

If you can't sign in, or you only commented on a creator's post

If you commented on a creator who uses Kreatorkit and want us to delete your comment data and messages:

  1. Email kreatorkit.app@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Include your Instagram username (and the creator's username if you know it), or the email of your Kreatorkit account.
  3. We confirm by email and delete the data within 7 days, then send you a confirmation code for your records.

Remove Kreatorkit from the platform itself

  • Instagram: Settings > Website permissions > Apps and websites > Kreatorkit > Remove.
  • Facebook: Settings & privacy > Settings > Apps and websites > Kreatorkit > Remove.
  • Google / YouTube: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions > Kreatorkit > Remove access.

When you remove access this way, Kreatorkit can no longer act for you, and we delete the data we stored from that platform within 7 days.
