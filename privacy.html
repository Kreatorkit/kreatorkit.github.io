Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Kreatorkit ("we", "us") is a tool for content creators. It answers comments with direct messages, sends guide pages, builds link-in-bio pages and posts your videos to the platforms you connect. Kreatorkit is run by ABHIRAM.K, a sole proprietorship registered in India (Udyam registered). This policy explains what data we collect, why, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and how you can delete it.

By creating a Kreatorkit account or connecting a platform, you agree to this policy. If we change how we use your data in a way this policy doesn't cover, we will ask you to agree again before we do it.

1. Data we collect

Your Kreatorkit account

  • Your name and email address, and your password (stored only as a secure hash) or the account you sign in with.
  • Your plan and billing status. Payments are handled by our payment provider; we never see or store your full card number.
  • If you join the waitlist: only your email address.

When you connect Instagram or Facebook (Meta)

You connect with Meta's own login screen; we never see your Instagram or Facebook password. With the permissions you approve, we access:

  • Your Instagram professional account ID, username, profile picture and follower count.
  • Your posts and reels (ID, caption, link, time) so you can choose which ones get an automatic reply.
  • Comments on your posts: the comment text, its ID and time, and the commenter's username and Instagram-scoped ID. We use these only to check for your keyword and to reply.
  • Messages in conversations that start from your auto-reply (for example when someone taps "I followed"), and whether that person follows you. Instagram only shares this after the person has messaged you.
  • Access tokens that let Kreatorkit act for your account. These are stored encrypted.

When you connect YouTube (Google)

Kreatorkit uses YouTube API Services. You connect with Google's own login screen. With the permissions you approve, we access:

  • Your YouTube channel ID, name and picture, and a list of your latest uploads (title, link, thumbnail) so you can add them to your pages (read-only access).
  • The ability to upload videos you choose and set their title, description, privacy, scheduled publish time and thumbnail.
  • Basic information about the videos we uploaded for you (ID, status, link).
  • Access and refresh tokens, stored encrypted.

We do not access your YouTube watch history, subscriptions, comments, analytics or private messages, and we cannot change or delete videos you did not upload through Kreatorkit.

Content you create in Kreatorkit

  • Guide pages, picks pages, link-in-bio pages, reply texts, keywords and the videos and images you upload to post.

Visitors to pages made with Kreatorkit

  • We count taps on links and smart links: which link, the time, which app it was opened from (for example Instagram) and the device type (Android, iPhone or computer). We don't store IP addresses for this, don't use advertising cookies and don't build profiles of visitors.

People who buy from a creator

  • Payments are made on the payment company's own page (for example Stripe) straight into the creator's account. We never see or store card or bank details, and Kreatorkit doesn't hold the money.
  • We store the order: what was bought, the price, the time, the email address the payment company gives us, and a private download link.
  • The creator can see these orders. To connect a payment account a creator gives us a restricted key that can only start checkouts; we store it encrypted.

People who join a creator's list

  • If you join a creator's list on a page made with Kreatorkit, we store the email address and/or WhatsApp number you enter, the page you joined from, the time, and the exact words you agreed to.
  • You join only by ticking an unticked "I agree" box that says who will contact you, about what, and how.
  • The creator decides how they use their list (they are responsible for it as the data fiduciary); Kreatorkit stores it for them and does not use it for anything else.

2. How we use data

  • To run the features you turn on: replying to comments, sending DMs and guides, the follow gate, posting and scheduling videos, and showing your stats.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent spam and abuse, and fix problems.
  • To contact you about your account, billing and important changes.

We never sell personal data. We never use it for advertising. We never send automated messages to anyone who has not commented on or messaged your account first.

Google and YouTube data (Limited Use). Kreatorkit's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use YouTube data only to provide the features you see in Kreatorkit. We do not sell it, do not use it for advertising, do not let humans read it except with your permission, for security, or when the law requires, and do not use it to train or improve AI or machine-learning models.

By using Kreatorkit's YouTube features you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Who we share data with

  • The platforms you connect (Meta/Instagram, Google/YouTube), only to do what you asked, such as sending a reply or uploading a video.
  • Service providers that host and run Kreatorkit (servers, databases, email delivery, payments). They may only use the data to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

4. How long we keep data

  • Comment and message data used for auto-replies: up to 90 days, then deleted. Meta only allows a private reply within 7 days of a comment, so we don't need it longer.
  • Access tokens: until you disconnect the platform or delete your account.
  • Your pages, settings and stats: until you delete them or your account.
  • Waitlist emails: until we open or you ask us to remove yours.
  • A creator's list entries: until you remove yourself, the creator removes you, or the creator deletes their account.
  • Orders: until the creator deletes their account (payment companies keep their own records under their own rules).
  • Backups are deleted within 30 days.

5. Removing access and deleting your data

  • Disconnect a platform in Kreatorkit settings at any time. We stop using it immediately and delete its tokens and data within 7 days.
  • Google/YouTube: you can also remove Kreatorkit's access on Google's security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. We then delete the YouTube data we stored within 7 days.
  • Instagram/Facebook: you can remove Kreatorkit in Instagram (Settings > Website permissions > Apps and websites) or Facebook (Settings > Apps and websites).
  • Delete everything: follow the steps on our Data Deletion page.
  • Leave a creator's list: use the "Remove me" link you get after joining (creators include it with their messages). It deletes your entry at once. You can also email us and we will remove you within 7 days.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Tokens are stored encrypted. Only the people who run Kreatorkit can access the systems, and only to operate and support the service.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or complain about how we handle it. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can also nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. Contact our grievance contact below; we reply within 7 days.

8. Children

Kreatorkit is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
