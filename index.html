You asked. We answered.

Do you take a cut of my sales?
No. Kreatorkit takes 0%. Buyers pay into your own payment account. The payment company's own fee (cards, UPI) applies, as it does everywhere.
Why do you ask for a card for a free trial?
So your founding price is locked and nothing stops on day 31. You see a reminder before the first charge, and you can cancel from your Plan page any time before it, for nothing.
When does Instagram comment to DM arrive?
It's built and waiting on Meta's approval for our app. We can't promise Meta's timing. Founding members get it at no extra cost the day it switches on.
Can I sell from India?
Selling works today with Stripe for creators outside India. UPI and Indian cards are next. Your pages, list, classes and scheduling work everywhere now.
What happens after 100 founding seats?
New creators pay the normal price (₹499 / ₹1,499). Founding members keep their price for as long as they stay subscribed.
Is my data safe?
We use the official Instagram, YouTube and payment APIs, store keys encrypted, and never sell data. You can delete everything from Settings.
