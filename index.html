You asked. We answered.

Is this allowed by Instagram?
Yes. We use Instagram's official API from Meta, and you connect with Meta's own login.
Why is it this cheap?
We built it for our own Instagram accounts first. $19 is the founding price, and founding creators help decide what we build next.
Do people really have to follow me?
Only if you switch on the follow gate. They follow, tap "I followed", and your guide arrives.
Does my computer need to stay on?
No. Kreatorkit runs online, so replies go out while you sleep.
Do you take a cut of my sales?
No. You sell with your own payment link and keep all of it, on every plan.
What about YouTube?
Smart YouTube links are part of the kit. Posting your reels to YouTube Shorts is on the way.
